## Next Steps

After the Symbol Mobile Wallet is installed, you need to create or import a wallet so you can keep your accounts organized.

* To set up a new wallet and account, follow the [Creating a Wallet](./create-wallet.md) guide.
* To restore a wallet and its seed accounts, follow the [Importing a Wallet](./import-wallet.md) guide.
* To restore an external account from its <private key:>, follow the [Importing an Account](./import-account.md) guide.
